Strategic Alignment · March 2026

Fueling the
E20–E27
Transition

India achieved the E20 ethanol blending milestone in November 2025 — four months ahead of schedule. As of February 2026, the 20% blending level has been sustained continuously for four consecutive months, with OMCs receiving 88.5 crore litres in February alone.

The national conversation has already moved to the post-E20 roadmap. ISMA formally approached the PMO in March 2026 urging a fast-tracked E22→E25→E27 roadmap. BIS has been directed to develop E27 fuel standards. For a plant commissioning in 2028, this trajectory is a direct demand tailwind.

Strategic Location

The Border Advantage

Bellary, Karnataka — at the intersection of two states' agricultural surplus and industrial incentives.

Key Distances

🌾 Raichur AMC: ~60 km
🌽 Kurnool AMC: ~80 km
🏭 Hospet Rail: ~25 km
01
📍 Bellary District, Karnataka
Dual-State Agricultural Access
Bellary sits at the heart of a region straddling both Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Maize from Bellary, Raichur, and Koppal (Karnataka) and rice/maize from Kurnool and Anantapur (AP) — all within 100 km. No single-state supply dependency.
02
🚂 Railway Freight Corridor
Logistics Infrastructure
Hospet Railway Junction (~25 km) and Guntakal (~55 km) provide bulk grain inflow and ethanol dispatch capability. NH-67 connects to all OMC depots. Guntakal is a major petroleum product distribution node — ideal for ethanol offtake.
03
💧 Tungabhadra Command Area
Feedstock — The Rice Bowl
The Tungabhadra command area (Bellary, Raichur, Sindhanur, Koppal) generates massive broken rice volumes from 300+ rice mills. Broken rice at ₹22-26/kg, local and abundant. Combined with Bellary's own maize arrivals, the plant has a near-unmatched multi-feedstock base.
04
🏭 OMC Deficit Zone Premium
Allocation Advantage
South India is a structural ethanol production deficit zone. Even with ESY 2025-26 showing 1.7x national tender oversubscription, OMC allocation policy actively favors regional balance. South Indian plants receive preferential allocation — a structural competitive moat.
Dry Milling — Industry Standard

Plant Technical
Architecture

1
🌾
Grain Milling
10,000 MT silos (30-45 day buffer). NIR moisture/starch testing on every lot. 2×15 TPH Hammer Mills to <1mm flour.
Throughput: ~154 MT/day
2
🔥
Liquefaction
Slurry mixed with recycle water + α-amylase. Jet Cooked at 105-110°C for full starch gelatinization.
Temp: 105-110°C · 3-5 min
3
🧫
SSF Fermentation
Glucoamylase saccharification → Saccharomyces cerevisiae yeast fermentation. Simultaneous process over 48-52 hours.
8-10% v/v wash · 2×500 KL
4
🏛️
Distillation
Multipressure system: Analyzer → Degassing → Rectifier columns. Produces Rectified Spirit at 95% v/v.
RS: 95% v/v output
5
🔬
Dehydration
Molecular Sieve (MSDH/PSA). Twin Zeolite beds alternating adsorption-regeneration cycle to remove residual water.
99.8% v/v — Fuel Grade
6
♻️
ZLD + DDGS
Decanter → MEE (5-7 effect) → DDGS Steam Dryer. CPU: UASB + Aerobic + RO. 70% process water recycled.
